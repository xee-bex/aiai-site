Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-25

What is read from a linked brokerage account

With read-only authorisation, the app reads the following from the linked account: account id and status, cash, equity and buying power, positions, orders and fills, and account activity. It does not read or store your brokerage password, identity documents, bank details or tax identifiers, and it has no access to them.

Where it is stored

All data read from the account is written to plain files on the owner's own computer, which is encrypted with full-disk encryption. The access token is stored in a local configuration file on that computer, readable by the owner only. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app's author; there is no such server.

What is shared

Nothing from the account is sent to any third party. The AI models the committee uses receive the questions put to them and public market information; they do not receive account identifiers, balances or positions.

Analytics and tracking

None. This site and the app set no cookies and run no analytics.

Retention and deletion

Local files are kept until the owner deletes them. Revoking the app from the connected-apps page of the brokerage dashboard ends its access immediately; deleting the local configuration file removes the stored token.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the app's owner at the contact address given on the brokerage's app listing.
