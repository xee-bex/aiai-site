Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-24

What is read from Alpaca

With read-only authorisation, the application reads the following from the connected Alpaca account: account id and status, cash, equity and buying power, positions, orders and fills, and account activity. It does not read or store your Alpaca password, identity documents, bank details or tax identifiers, and it has no access to them.

Where it is stored

All data read from Alpaca is written to plain files on the owner's own computer. The OAuth access token is stored in a local configuration file on that computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the application's authors; there is no such server.

What is shared

Nothing from the Alpaca account is sent to any third party. The AI models the committee uses receive the questions put to them and public market information; they do not receive account identifiers, balances or positions.

Analytics and tracking

None. This site and the application set no cookies and run no analytics.

Retention and deletion

Local files are kept until the owner deletes them. Revoking the application from the Connected Apps page of the Alpaca dashboard ends its access immediately; deleting the local configuration file removes the stored token.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the application's owner at the contact address given on the Alpaca Connect listing.
