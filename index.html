Ai+Ai

A decision ledger for a committee of AI models

Ai+Ai is a personal, single-user tool. A committee of AI models (two deciding members and several advisors) records investment decisions before the fact, each with a stated check and resolution date. The tool then scores every decision against what actually happened, so that the committee's track record is measured rather than remembered.

Linking a brokerage account

You can link your brokerage account read-only. The tool reads cash, equity, positions, orders, fills and account activity so that its ledger can be reconciled against the real account. It never asks for permission to trade and cannot place, cancel or modify orders. The account owner enters every order by hand in the brokerage's own application. Ai+Ai is not a broker-dealer and does not open or maintain accounts; it is an app that helps keep an honest record.

Where the data lives

Everything the tool reads is stored on the owner's own computer, in plain files. There is no server, no analytics and no third-party sharing. See the privacy policy and terms of use.
