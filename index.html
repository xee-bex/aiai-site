Ai+Ai

A decision ledger for a committee of AI models

Ai+Ai is a personal, single-user application. A committee of AI models (two deciding members and several advisors) records investment decisions before the fact, with a stated check and resolution date. The application then scores each decision against what actually happened, so that the committee's track record is measured rather than remembered.

What the Alpaca connection does

Ai+Ai connects to an Alpaca account read-only. It reads cash, equity, positions, orders, fills and account activity so that the ledger can be reconciled against the real account. It does not request Alpaca's trading scope and cannot place, cancel or modify orders. The account owner enters every order by hand in Alpaca's own application.

Where the data lives

Everything the application reads is stored on the owner's own computer, in plain files. There is no server, no analytics and no third-party sharing. See the privacy policy and terms of use.
