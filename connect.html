Ai+Ai

Connect your Alpaca account

Ai+Ai reads your paper account so its decision ledger can be reconciled against what the account actually did. It never places orders.

Authorize Ai+Ai

By allowing Ai+Ai to access your Alpaca account, you are granting Ai+Ai access to your account information and authorization to place transactions in your account at your direction. Alpaca does not warrant or guarantee that Ai+Ai will work as advertised or expected. Before authorizing, learn more about Ai+Ai.
  • Requested: read account, positions, orders, fills and activity
  • Not requested: trading scope — Ai+Ai cannot place, cancel or modify orders
