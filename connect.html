Ai+Ai

Link your brokerage account

Ai+Ai reads the account so its decision ledger can be reconciled against what the account actually did. It never places orders.

Authorize Ai+Ai

By allowing Ai+Ai to access your Alpaca account, you are granting Ai+Ai access to your account information and authorization to place transactions in your account at your direction. Alpaca does not warrant or guarantee that Ai+Ai will work as advertised or expected. Before authorizing, learn more about Ai+Ai.
  • Requested: read account, positions, orders, fills and activity
  • Not requested: permission to trade — Ai+Ai cannot place, cancel or modify orders
Continue to your brokerage
The button unlocks after the acknowledgment. You will be sent to your brokerage to approve or decline, and can revoke the link at any time under its connected apps.
